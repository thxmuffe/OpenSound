Known node names

    The reception desk keeps node names, not chat messages. Names stay until it restarts. This is not a live connection check; no cookies are stored or exchanged.

POST /api/nodes
Content-Type: application/json
{"node_id":"mac@192.168.1.10"}

Response:
{"node_ids":["mac@192.168.1.10"],
 "registered_node_id":"mac@192.168.1.10"}

    The app filters out its own name and attempts handshakes with reachable peers using separately configured credentials.
